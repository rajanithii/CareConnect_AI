
### Blog Post Strategy

**Publish Schedule:** 2-4 posts per month

**Content Types:**

1. **Thought Leadership** (Monthly)
   - Topic: Industry trends, predictions
   - Length: 1,500+ words
   - Target: Hospital decision makers
   - Example: "The Future of AI in Healthcare"

2. **How-To Guides** (2x per month)
   - Topic: Practical hospital blood management tips
   - Length: 1,000-1,500 words
   - Target: Hospital staff
   - Example: "5-Step Emergency Blood Protocol"

3. **Case Studies** (Monthly)
   - Topic: Real hospital success stories
   - Length: 1,000-1,200 words
   - Target: Prospective hospitals
   - Metrics: specific numbers

4. **Educational Content** (Monthly)
   - Topic: Blood banking fundamentals
   - Length: 1,200-1,500 words
   - Target: General awareness

### Blog Post Template
```
Title: "[Action/Number] [Topic] [Benefit]"
Example: "5 Ways to Reduce Blood Waste in Hospitals"

Intro (100 words):
- Hook: Statistic or question
- Problem statement
- Why it matters
- What readers will learn

Body Section 1 (200 words):
H2: First point title
- Explanation
- Example
- Benefit

[Repeat for 4-5 more sections]

Conclusion (100 words):
- Summary of key points
- Call to action
- Next steps

Meta:
- Word count: 1,200-1,500
- Keywords: 2-3 primary, 4-6 secondary
- Internal links: 2-3
- External links: 2-3
- Images: 2-3 with alt text
```
